Individual Tree Crown Detection Architecture

Individual tree crowns require sub-meter high-resolution RGB imagery (< 1m/px) processed with the DeepForest RetinaNet neural network. Sentinel-1 & 2 (10–20m) cannot resolve individual tree crowns.
